# Hi, I'm Jiamao Liu 👋

**Computational Biology · Spatial Multi-omics · Epigenomics · Gene Regulation**

I am an M.S. student in Bioinformatics at **Xiamen University**, interested in understanding gene regulation through **single-cell and spatial multi-omics**.

## 🧬 Selected Research

### Spatial enhancer regulation

Developing computational approaches to understand how combinations of enhancers encode spatial gene-expression patterns.

**Keywords:** spatial transcriptomics · chromatin accessibility · enhancer–gene links · gene regulation

### Spatial epigenomics inference

Integrating spatial transcriptomics with single-cell multiome or scATAC-seq data to reconstruct spatial chromatin accessibility and regulatory landscapes.

**Keywords:** spatial ATAC inference · multi-modal integration · regulatory genomics

### Human spinal cord development

Studying the spatiotemporal and epigenetic principles underlying human spinal cord development using single-nucleus Multiome and Stereo-seq spatial transcriptomics.

**Keywords:** human development · snRNA-seq · snATAC-seq · Stereo-seq · GWAS
